Froppy Frog help

Support

Help with purchases, progress, offline play, ads, notifications, and content preferences.

A purchase did not arrive

Tap Restore purchases at Shop › Special or Settings › Account. If the game says approval is pending through Family Sharing, the pack arrives automatically after approval. An internet connection is required.

I moved to a new iPhone. Where is my progress?

If iCloud is enabled with the same Apple Account on both devices, progress syncs automatically. The permanent part of Ad-free Froppy, Golden Froppy, Golden Pack, Richy Frog, and Infinity—such as a costume or ad-free status—also returns through Restore purchases.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Core gameplay works offline: levels, lives, boosters, and your save stay available on the device. Rewarded-ad bonuses—including Continue, +1 Life, and doubled rewards—remain visible offline. If you tap one, the game asks you to connect; after reconnecting, choose “I’m connected, try again” to continue the same reward flow. No reward is granted before the connection returns.

If an interstitial becomes due while you are offline, the game may show at most one later, after you reconnect and reach a safe break. Purchases and Game Center also require a connection.

Ads and ad privacy

With an ad-free pack, interstitial ads do not appear and eligible rewards are granted without watching an ad. In Europe, you can change your ad consent choice at Settings › Ad privacy options.

Notifications

The game sends at most one reminder a day and none between 22:00 and 09:00. Turn reminders off at Settings › Notifications.

Festival content

Turn individual holiday decorations and gifts on or off at Settings › Content preferences.
